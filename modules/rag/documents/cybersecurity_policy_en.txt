All employees must use strong passwords with at least 12 characters including uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days and cannot be reused for 5 previous passwords.
Multi-factor authentication MFA is mandatory for all company accounts and remote access.
Employees must not share their credentials with anyone including IT support staff.
Phishing emails should be reported immediately to the security team at security@company.com.
Unauthorized software installation on company devices is strictly prohibited.
All company data must be stored on approved cloud platforms only.
USB drives and external storage devices are not permitted on company premises.
VPN must be used when accessing company resources from outside the office network.
Data breaches must be reported to the IT security team within 1 hour of discovery.
