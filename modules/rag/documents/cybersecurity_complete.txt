All employees must use strong passwords with at least 12 characters including uppercase lowercase numbers and symbols.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days and cannot be reused for the last 5 previous passwords.
Multi-factor authentication MFA is mandatory for all company accounts and all remote access connections.
Employees must never share their credentials with anyone including IT support staff and managers.
Phishing emails must be reported immediately to the security team at security@company.com without clicking any links.
Unauthorized software installation on company devices is strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action.
All company data must be stored exclusively on approved cloud platforms and never on personal devices.
USB drives and external storage devices are strictly forbidden on all company computers and workstations.
VPN must always be used when accessing company resources from outside the office network or from home.
Data breaches must be reported to the IT security team within 1 hour of discovery without exception.
Personal mobile devices used for work must be enrolled in the Mobile Device Management MDM system.
Company laptops must be encrypted using BitLocker or FileVault and locked when unattended.
Social engineering attacks like pretexting and baiting must be reported to the security team immediately.
All sensitive emails containing personal or financial data must be encrypted before sending externally.
Security awareness training is mandatory for all employees and must be completed within 30 days of hiring.
Access to sensitive systems must follow the principle of least privilege and be reviewed every 6 months.
Incident response procedures must be initiated within 15 minutes of detecting a potential security breach.
All third-party vendors accessing company systems must sign a data processing agreement before access.
Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing are conducted quarterly on all critical systems.
Employees working remotely must ensure their home network uses WPA3 encryption and a strong password.
